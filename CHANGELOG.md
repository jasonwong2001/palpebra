# Changelog

All notable changes to Palpebra are documented here, following [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-27

The first public release.

### Added

- Reminders based on eye fatigue: using your Mac fills a meter, resting empties it, and a break starts at 100%.
- Three reminder styles: a full-screen break on every display, a small floating reminder, or a macOS notification, with an optional warning a minute before.
- Small reminders and notifications finish once you've actually rested. A full-screen break ends with its countdown.
- Skip freely, solve a quick puzzle to skip, or make breaks unskippable. In an emergency, hold Esc for five seconds.
- Locking your Mac or letting it sleep counts as rest, overnight included.
- Smart detection goes by how long it's been since you last touched the keyboard, mouse or trackpad, so the only permission Palpebra asks for is notifications. Videos and calls can count as screen time too.
- Today shows your fatigue, the next break, screen time and breaks taken. Analysis charts screen time, time at high fatigue and completed breaks by day, week or month.
- Optional notifications when a day's screen time passes two limits you choose.
- A global Rest now shortcut, pauses from 30 minutes to the rest of the day, a fatigue ring on the Dock icon, fatigue or screen time in the menu bar, and Open at login.
- Type any value for timings and brightness, within each setting's range. Choose the full-screen background (including your own image), its brightness and message, and a sound when a break ends.
- Your history stays on your Mac. Export it as CSV or clear it in Settings. There's no account, analytics or network access.
- VoiceOver descriptions for fatigue, charts and countdowns, and keyboard control of every break.
- One app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs on macOS 13 or later. It's ad-hoc signed and not notarized; the README explains how to open it the first time.
